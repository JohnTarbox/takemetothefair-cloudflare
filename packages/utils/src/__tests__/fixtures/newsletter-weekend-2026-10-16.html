Good morning — and happy third weekend of October. It is peak leaf-peeping, apple-pressing, pumpkin-carving season across New England, and the calendar shows it: this weekend (Friday, October 16 through Sunday the 18th) we are tracking 40 fairs, festivals and markets from Caribou down to Mystic. Here are a dozen we would not want you to miss, followed by the complete list, state by state, so you can find the one nearest you.

This Weekend’s Picks

A dozen standouts, strongest first.

Head of the Charles Regatta

Regatta · Fri, Oct 16 – Sun, 18 · Boston, MA

The world’s largest three-day rowing event brings roughly 11,500 rowers to the Charles River course from the BU boathouse to Herter Park, drawing around 325,000 spectators, plus a Rower’s Village vendor expo. Free to watch; reserved areas up to $25.

Wellfleet OysterFest

Food · Sat, Oct 17 – Sun, 18 · Wellfleet, MA

Cape Cod’s 26th oyster celebration takes over downtown Wellfleet with raw bars, restaurant tents, a craft vendor village, live music, the storied Tasting Tent and the oyster-shucking contest. Free to enter; Tasting Tent and events ticketed (up to $35).

Bowen’s Wharf Seafood Festival

Food · Sat, Oct 17 – Sun, 18 · Newport, RI

The 35th annual festival fills Newport’s historic waterfront with fresh local seafood, live music and Rhode Island shellfish growers under the wharf’s signature striped tents. Free admission; food and drink for purchase from Newport restaurants.

Glastonbury Apple Harvest & Music Festival

Festival · Fri, Oct 16 – Sun, 18 · Glastonbury, CT · dates to confirm

Connecticut’s premier fall celebration draws 100,000+ over three days with live music on multiple stages, amusement rides, food vendors, curated craft booths, a Harvest Pub and a 5K. Dates are still to be confirmed — check the organizer before you go.

Harvest on the Harbor

Food · Thu, Oct 15 – Sat, 17 · Portland, ME

Portland’s food-and-drink festival gathers Maine’s best restaurants, brewers, distillers and cheesemakers, with the Maine Lobster Chef of the Year and Oyster Shucking competitions and plenty of tastings. Ticketed events, $17.59–$129.95.

Mystic Apple Festival

Festival · Sat, Oct 17 – Sun, 18 · Mystic, CT

Olde Mistick Village’s fifth apple festival brings artisan vendors and craft booths, seasonal treats from pumpkin to maple to apple, live music and family activities to the open-air shopping village. Free admission.

Keene Pumpkin Festival

Festival · Sat, Oct 17 · Keene, NH

Thousands of carved jack-o’-lanterns line downtown Keene’s Main Street for this beloved one-day festival, with live music, food vendors and family-friendly activities. Free admission.

Fabulous Fall Craft & Artisan Show

Craft · Sat, Oct 17 – Sun, 18 · Milford, NH

The Great New England Craft & Artisan Shows’ biggest show of the year packs 165 exhibitors into the Hampshire Dome for two days of juried handmade shopping, with live music, food trucks, a café and craft brews. $7, good both days; 14 and under free.

Leaf Peeper’s Craft Fair

Craft · Sat, Oct 17 – Sun, 18 · North Conway, NH

More than 120 arts-and-crafts exhibitors fill Schouler Park in downtown North Conway, with chainsaw wood-carving demos, pottery, maple syrup, homemade fudge and live music from North River both days. Sat 10–5, Sun 10–4; rain or shine under canopies.

York Harvestfest

Craft · Sat, Oct 17 · York, ME

The York Chamber’s signature fall festival sets up at Short Sands Beach, 9am–4pm, with a juried craft market of 100+ makers, food trucks, live music, family activities and a Kidsfest. Free admission and parking, rain or shine.

Caribou Craft Fair

Craft · Sat, Oct 17 – Sun, 18 · Caribou, ME · dates to confirm

Maine’s longest-running craft fair, now in its sixth decade, brings 140+ artisans to the County with woodcraft, pottery, textiles, jewelry and holiday décor — ideal early holiday shopping. Free admission; dates are projected from prior years, so confirm with the City of Caribou.

Bennington Harvest Fest

Harvest · Sat, Oct 17 · Bennington, VT

Better Bennington’s fifth Harvest Fest fills downtown Main Street 10am–5pm with 100+ vendors, food trucks on School Street, a day of live music, a Kid Zone in Merchants Park and historical reenactments. Free admission.

Everything This Weekend, State by State

All 40 events in this weekend’s window, grouped by state and listed by date.

Maine (9)

Thu, Oct 15 – Sat, 17 · Portland — Harvest on the Harbor (Food). Portland’s waterfront food-and-drink festival, with the Maine Lobster Chef of the Year and Oyster Shucking competitions. Ticketed events, $17.59–$129.95.

Sat, Oct 17 – Sun, 18 · New Gloucester — Artisans & Apples Fall Fair (Craft). 50+ Maine vendors among the foliage at Thompson’s Orchard; opens 9am Sat, closes 3pm Sun, with pick-your-own apples, tractor rides, food trucks and live music. Free.

Sat, Oct 17 · Waterville — AYCC Annual Craft Fair (Craft). Handmade goods, home décor, gifts and baked goods at the Alfond Youth & Community Center. Free admission.

Sat, Oct 17 – Sun, 18 · Caribou — Caribou Craft Fair (Craft). Featured above — Maine’s longest-running craft fair, 140+ artisans. Free; dates to confirm.

Sat, Oct 17 · Auburn — Great Falls Model Railroad Club October Train Show (Trains). Model trains in N, HO, S, O and G scales at Edward Little High School, 10am–3pm, with operating layouts and dealers. $5, under 12 free; date to confirm.

Sat, Oct 17 – Sun, 18 · Bangor — PCCA Annual Bangor Gun Show (Expo). Firearms, ammunition, knives and militaria from dealers and collectors at the Bangor Elks Club; Sat 9–5, Sun 9–2. $8 general; date to confirm.

Sat, Oct 17 · Standish — Standish Makers Market — October (Makers). Monthly outdoor makers market at Hometown Furnishing Co., 9am–3pm, with local artisans, crafters and vendors.

Sat, Oct 17 · York — York Harvestfest (Craft). Featured above — 100+ makers at Short Sands Beach, 9am–4pm. Free admission and parking.

Sun, Oct 18 · Bangor — Bangor Sunday Farmers Market (Market). Sundays 11am–1:30pm at Abbot Square, across from the Bangor Public Library; Penobscot County produce, bread, eggs, cheese, coffee and Maine specialty foods.

New Hampshire (8)

Sat, Oct 17 – Sun, 18 · Lee — Coppal House Farm Artisan Craft Fair (Craft). Roughly 35–40 craftspeople at the farm, both days 10am–5pm, alongside its fall agritourism. No admission fee; dates to confirm.

Sat, Oct 17 – Sun, 18 · Milford — Fabulous Fall Craft & Artisan Show (Craft). Featured above — 165 exhibitors at the Hampshire Dome. $7 both days, 14 & under free.

Sat, Oct 17 · Keene — Keene Pumpkin Festival (Festival). Featured above — thousands of jack-o’-lanterns on Main Street. Free.

Sat, Oct 17 · Laconia — Ladies of the Lake Fall Crafts Fair & Festival (Craft). 75+ juried crafters at Laconia High School, plus food trucks, a bake sale and the 9th annual Wreath Festival. Free; proceeds benefit the Children’s Auction.

Sat, Oct 17 – Sun, 18 · North Conway — Leaf Peeper’s Craft Fair (Craft). Featured above — 120+ exhibitors at Schouler Park. Sat 10–5, Sun 10–4.

Sat, Oct 17 · Gilford — Paugus Bay October Makers Market (Makers). Local artists and small businesses at The Village at Paugus Bay, with a costume contest, pumpkin carving and trick-or-treating through the booths.

Sat, Oct 17 · Berlin — RiverFire (Festival). The Androscoggin Valley Chamber’s fall festival: hayrides, train rides, live music, axe throwing, a costume parade and the signature Lighting of the Boompiers at dusk. $5, 5 & under free.

Sat, Oct 17 – Sun, 18 · Holderness — Squam Fest: An Enchanted Market (Market). An eclectic popup market at Cottage Place on Squam Lake — vintage treasures, handcrafted goods, jewelry, baked goods and artwork.

Vermont (7)

Fri, Oct 16 – Sun, 18 · Essex Junction — Champlain Valley Craft Show & Antique Expo (Craft). Three-day indoor juried craft and antique show at the Champlain Valley Exposition. $12 at the gate (good all three days, $2 off online), under 14 free; free parking, rain or shine.

Sat, Oct 17 · Bennington — Bennington Harvest Fest (Harvest). Featured above — 100+ vendors downtown, 10am–5pm. Free.

Sat, Oct 17 · Brattleboro — Brattleboro Area Farmers Market (Market). Saturdays 9am–2pm at 570 Western Ave; Windham County farmers, food producers and artisans, with live music 11am–1pm.

Sat, Oct 17 · Burlington — Burlington Summer Farmers Market (Market). Saturdays 9am–2pm at 345 Pine St in the South End; produce, prepared foods and crafters from across northwest Vermont.

Sat, Oct 17 · Montpelier — Capital City Farmers Market (Market). Saturdays 9am–1pm at 133 State St in downtown Montpelier; central Vermont’s premier outdoor market of farmers, food producers and artisans.

Sat, Oct 17 · South Burlington — PokeKon-Fest Burlington (Convention). An all-Pokémon show at the DoubleTree — TCG, retro items, toys and games, local artists, panels and a voice-actor guest. Date to confirm.

Sat, Oct 17 · Rutland — Rutland Downtown Summer Farmers Market (Market). Saturdays 9am–2pm at Depot Park; 60+ vendors with Vermont produce, meats, cheeses, baked goods and crafts.

Massachusetts (6)

Thu, Oct 1 – Sat, 31 · Salem — Salem Haunted Happenings (Festival). Salem’s month-long Halloween celebration, anchored at Salem Common with family activities, vendor markets, ghost tours and haunted attractions — in full swing this weekend, building toward the grand parade.

Fri, Oct 16 – Sun, 18 · Boston — Head of the Charles Regatta (Regatta). Featured above — the world’s largest three-day rowing event. Free to watch; reserved areas to $25.

Sat, Oct 17 – Sun, 18 · Newton — Newton Harvest Fair (Harvest). Newton Centre Green: Sat 10–5 (kiddie rides and food trucks), Sun 11–4 with full programming — 100+ crafters, a GreenEXPO, live music and free pumpkin decorating. Free.

Sat, Oct 17 · Rehoboth — Rehoboth Crafters’ Marketplace (Craft). A 46-year-old juried show across three walkable Rehoboth Village locations, with handmade jewelry, housewares, décor and small-batch edibles. Free entry and parking, rain or shine.

Sat, Oct 17 · Middleborough — Soule Homestead Harvest Fair (Harvest). Harvest fair at the historic Soule Homestead with artisan demonstrations, crafters, hayrides, a cranberry-bog exhibit, antique tractors and live music.

Sat, Oct 17 – Sun, 18 · Wellfleet — Wellfleet OysterFest (Food). Featured above — Cape Cod’s 26th oyster festival. Free to enter; Tasting Tent and events ticketed (to $35).

Connecticut (5)

Fri, Oct 16 – Sun, 18 · Glastonbury — Glastonbury Apple Harvest & Music Festival (Festival). Featured above — Connecticut’s premier fall festival, 100,000+ over three days. Dates to confirm.

Sun, Oct 18 – Sun, 25 · Warren — Warren Walkabout (Market). Warren’s downtown walkabout runs two October Sundays (the 18th and 25th), noon–5pm — live music, local art, independent shops, food and vendor pop-ups. Free.
