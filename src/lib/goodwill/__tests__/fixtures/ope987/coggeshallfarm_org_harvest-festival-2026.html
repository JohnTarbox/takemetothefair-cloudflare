Harvest Festival 2026

Join Coggeshall Farm Museum as we celebrate the culmination of our farming season. Enjoy this day-long event at our 18th-century coastal tenant farm and experience the rich heritage of the land and sea. Visitors will experience oxen team demonstrations and cider-making demonstrations, horse-drawn hay rides, and children’s games and activities. Experience locally made artisan crafts from Rhode Island and New England vendors. The event will also feature regional food and beverages along with food trucks and local musicians.
