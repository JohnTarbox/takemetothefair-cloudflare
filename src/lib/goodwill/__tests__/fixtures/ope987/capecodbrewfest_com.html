Cape Cod Brew Fest

2026 Festival Cancelled

After 14 incredible years, we have made the difficult decision to cancel the 2026 Cape Cod Brew Fest.

Over the past several years, attendance has steadily declined, and unfortunately ticket sales for this year’s event have not reached a level that allows us to produce the festival our guests have come to expect.

All tickets will be automatically refunded to the original method of payment. No action is required, and refunds will begin processing shortly.

We are incredibly grateful to everyone who attended, volunteered, exhibited, sponsored, and supported the Cape Cod Brew Fest over the past 14 years. Your enthusiasm helped make this event a Cape Cod tradition, and we sincerely appreciate being part of so many great memories.

Thank you for your support and understanding.

Location

The Cape Cod Fairgrounds hosted to the Cape Cod Brew Fest since its inaugural event in 2012 and provided the perfect indoor/outdoor venue for a fall beer festival.
