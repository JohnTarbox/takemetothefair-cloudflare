A cherished Connecticut tradition for all ages

Established in 1916, the Durham Fair is one of the largest volunteer-run fairs in North America. Rooted in agriculture and community pride, it blends the authentic charm of New England farm life with the excitement of live entertainment, heart-pounding events, and the food you dream about all year long.
