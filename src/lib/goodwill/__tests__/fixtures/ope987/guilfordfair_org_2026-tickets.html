Tickets and On-Site Parking

Adults/Children 12 & up – $15.00
Senior Citizens (*62 & up) – $10.00
Veterans & current Military (with ID) – $10.00
Children 11 & under – Free
Parking On Site – $10.00


ATMs available at Main Fair Entrance.

Shuttle Bus with Free Parking

Reduce stress and save! Free Parking with Shuttle Bus service to the Fairgrounds is available via the satellite parking lot, located at 1575 Boston Post Road, Guilford, just off of I-95/Exit 57. Please click here for more info ->

Ride Tickets & Wristbands

Full Midway and Rides by Rockwell Amusements. Get detailed information about ride tickets and wristbands here ->

PLEASE NOTE: Outside alcohol is prohibited on the Fairgrounds and in the Fair parking lots. Alcoholic beverage purchases and consumption are permitted with ID bracelets from the Thimble Island Brewery tent inside the Fairgrounds.

The following items may not be brought into the Fairgrounds: Coolers, Backpacks, Beverage and Water bottles or containers. All bags are subject to search.

Note: PETS are not permitted on the Fairgrounds, including the Parking Lots.

The Guilford Fairgrounds are handicapped accessible, and accessible parking is available at THE FAIRGOUNDS ON-SITE PARKING LOT ONLY. Please note that the shuttle buses/remote parking lot do not provide handicapped accessibility.

Performances & Showtimes are subject to change and/or cancellation due to weather or other unforseen issues. No refunds are given due to such circumstances.
