Buy, Sell, Trade, and Explore

Eastern Gun Expo events are built for people who want more than just a quick walk-through. Attendees come to shop firearms, ammunition, knives, optics, accessories, military surplus, outdoor gear, collectibles, and specialty products from vendors across Pennsylvania and beyond.

At every event, you can:

  • Buy firearms, ammo, knives, and gear
  • Sell or trade eligible items
  • Meet trusted vendors and fellow enthusiasts
  • Explore products for hunting, sport shooting, collecting, and outdoor use

Why Visitors Choose Eastern Gun Expo

We know attendees want clear information, quality vendors, and a smooth event experience. That is why Eastern Gun Expo focuses on well-organized shows, convenient venues, and a strong mix of exhibitors that give visitors a reason to come back show after show.

  • Pennsylvania-focused event schedule
  • Family-friendly show environment
  • Strong vendor participation
  • Easy access to dates, tickets, and venue details
  • VIP early entry available at select events
