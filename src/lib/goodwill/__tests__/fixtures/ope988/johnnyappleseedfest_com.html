FAMILY FESTIVAL HOURS:  SATURDAY 10AM – 6PM      SUNDAY 10AM – 5PM

We hope you’ll come out and enjoy a wonderful family festival event at the largest festival in Fort Wayne.  Step back in time to Johnny’s day around 1842 and smell, see and enjoy all the sights and sounds that could have been around during Johnny’s  lifetime.  There is no entrance fee to get into this family friendly festival.  There is no alcohol sold at the festival and we ask that you leave your family pet at home – for their comfort and safety.

Our over 200 vendors come from all around and for hundreds of miles just be participate and share what they have to offer.   Most of our food vendors are Not-For-Profit organizations all using our festival as their own biggest fundraiser for themselves.  Help support our local schools, churches and organizations!

Our Mission Statement

Celebrating the Life and Times of John Chapman aka Johnny Appleseed

To educate and stimulate historical interest among the general public in order to promote a better understanding of Johnny Appleseed, his life and times. To provide a historically authentic recreation of the time of Johnny Appleseed and the period in which he lived and the plan, operate and administer the annual Johnny Appleseed Festival in Fort Wayne, Indiana.
We are an approved 501(c)(3) organization.

Our 2-Day family festival event offers a wide variety of family friendly shopping, music, food and more:

  • Handmade Crafts
  • 1800 Period Demonstrations
  • Children's Games, Mazes & Animals
  • Antiques & Collectibles
  • Trappers & Traders Living On-Site
  • Farmers Market
  • Food, Food & More Food!

OUR FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Here are our most frequently asked questions from both festival visitors and those interested in being a vendor.

01. How many days is it and when is the festival

The Johnny Appleseed Festival is a 2-day family festival event held on a Saturday and Sunday.  It will always be the THIRD FULL weekend in September.  The dates always change, but the weekend will not.  The event is held rain or shine.

02. Can anyone be a vendor

Our event is by invitation only - but any business, not for profit or individual can apply so long as you can comply with our 1800 period rules.  There is no modern booths, power or conveniences.  Read our vendor guidelines to learn more. WE DO NOT SOLICIT VENDOR APPLICATIONS VIA FACEBOOK POSTS.

03. Can I bring my pet to the festival

Your personal pets are not permitted at our festival.  We ask you to leave your dog, cat, ferret, snake, parrot, ostrich, gorilla, spider or any other pet you have at home where it is safe for them.  Yes, this event is at a public park, but the restriction is in place during the festival and patrolling Fort Wayne Police can issue you a ticket if you do not comply.

04. What are the parking options

There are several parking options available.  You can  park for FREE and ride our free shuttle service from the Purdue Fort Wayne parking area or Concordia High School Athletic Facility.  Buses run 1 hour before and after the festival hours each day.  You can also use the Memorial Coliseum and pay $8 for parking (JAF receives no financial funds from Coliseum parking)

05. What are the accessibility options

This is an outdoor family festival event and the grounds are a variety of grass, stone, asphalt and wood chips.  You may bring your own mobility scooter or wheelchair - both have worked well that we have seen.  We do not rent scooters or wheelchairs.  We do have handicap parking options close to the festival grounds.
